Sustainability

From the way we dye our fabrics to the way we make and pack every garment, sustainability and social responsibility always come first.

  • 01Environmental impactNatural, low-water fibres
  • 02Lowering emissionsAction across the value chain

We are committed to the environment

We use eco-friendly practices across our manufacturing processes, from the energy that powers our floors to the materials we pack your products in.

  • Energy-efficient facilities
  • Renewable energy from solar panels
  • Sustainable packaging materials

Environmental impact reduction

Fashion is one of the most polluting industries in the world. The first step towards a solution is awareness and the willingness to change, so we look closely at the materials and processes we use.

  • Organic and natural fibres that need no chemicals to grow
  • Low-water fibres such as linen, hemp and recycled fibres
  • Washing at 30°C to control microfibre pollution

Lowering emissions

Climate change makes cutting carbon urgent, and fashion is a sizeable contributor. We looked at the whole value chain, from farms and factories to brands and retailers, and found three areas to act on.

  • Upstream operations: fibre, yarn and fabric production
  • Our own operations: efficient machinery and cleaner energy
  • Brand partnerships: helping brands cut their own emissions

Minimizing waste

Making and using textiles can leave waste and pollution behind. A circular approach and biodegradable materials help keep fabric out of landfill.

  • Biodegradable materials such as bamboo, hemp and organic cotton
  • New bio-based fibres from citrus rinds and soybeans
  • Made-to-fit production to avoid overproduction

Efficiency using resources

Efficient manufacturing means producing the most with the least input: fewer materials, less energy and less time for every order.

  • Production planning for every step and deadline
  • Automation to streamline repetitive tasks
  • Lean manufacturing to cut fabric offcuts
  • Renewable inputs from sugar, algae and biomass
