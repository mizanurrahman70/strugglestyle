    From purchase order to shipped cartons

    Follow your garments through eight clear stages, from costing and sampling to cutting, sewing and shipping. Our merchandising team plans every detail, our production floor builds it, and you approve each step with one dedicated contact.

    Pre-productionMerchandising team
    HandoffPP approved + materials in-house
    ProductionProduction floor
    Step 01 of 08

    Order & merchandising

    Your merchandiser takes the inquiry or purchase order and confirms styles, quantities, size ratio, target price and delivery date. Then they plan every milestone to that delivery date.

    What we do

    • Review your tech pack and bill of materials
    • Costing and price confirmation
    • Time & action calendar with milestone dates

    Your sign-off

    Purchase order, unit price and delivery date

    Step 02 of 08

    Design & tech pack

    Our design team turns your brief into production patterns graded across your size range. They also specify every trim, label and packaging detail.

    What we do

    • Pattern making and size grading
    • Trim, label and packaging specs
    • Colour standards and lab dips

    Your sign-off

    Lab dips and trim cards

    Step 03 of 08

    Sampling

    The sample section sews proto, fit and size-set samples until the garment is right. The approved pre-production sample becomes the standard the whole order is checked against.

    What we do

    • Proto and fit samples
    • Size-set samples across the range
    • Sealed pre-production (PP) sample

    Your sign-off

    Fit comments and PP sample approval

    Step 04 of 08

    Raw material sourcing

    We book fabric, lace, elastic, hooks and cup mouldings from vetted mills and suppliers. Everything is checked on arrival, before it reaches the cutting table.

    What we do

    • Bulk fabric and trim booking
    • Incoming fabric and trim inspection
    • Shade and shrinkage testing

    Your sign-off

    Bulk fabric shade and quality

    Step 05 of 08

    Cutting

    Markers are planned to waste as little fabric as possible. The fabric is relaxed, spread and cut, and the panels are bundled by size and shade so every garment matches.

    What we do

    • Marker planning for low fabric waste
    • Fabric relaxing, spreading and cutting
    • Bundles numbered by size and shade

    You receive

    Cutting report and photos

    Step 06 of 08

    Sewing

    Bundles go to two dedicated lines with 34 sewing machines (Juki, Brother, Yamato and Sew Power) and 5 moulding machines for cups. Quality checks run on every line while the order is sewn.

    What we do

    • Line balancing for your styles
    • Cup moulding and assembly
    • Inline quality checks on every line

    You receive

    Production photos and progress updates

    Step 07 of 08

    Finishing & quality

    Each garment is trimmed, pressed, measured and labelled, then folded and packed to your packing list. A final AQL inspection checks the order against the approved PP sample before it ships.

    What we do

    • Thread trimming, pressing and measurement checks
    • Labelling, folding and packing to your list
    • Final AQL inspection

    Your sign-off

    Final inspection report. Your own third-party inspector is welcome.

    Step 08 of 08

    Shipping & storage

    Packed cartons are stored dry until they ship. We handle booking and export documents, and the factory is only 21 km from Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport, which suits air shipments.

    What we do

    • Dry, secure carton storage
    • Freight booking by air or sea
    • Export documentation

    You receive

    Shipping documents and tracking
