      How an order comes together

      From your first brief to delivery at your port — one team guides every step.

      1. 01

        Merchandising

        We review your brief, tech pack and target price.

      2. 02

        Design

        Patterns are refined and key styles previewed in 3D.

      3. 03

        Sourcing

        Fabrics and trims from approved, traceable suppliers.

      4. 04

        Sampling

        Proto, fit and pre-production samples, approved with you.

      5. 05

        Production

        Lines planned against a confirmed schedule.

      6. 06

        Cutting

        Careful marker planning keeps fabric waste low.

      7. 07

        Sewing

        Dedicated lines with inline quality checks.

      8. 08

        Finishing

        Washing, pressing, final inspection and packing.

      9. 09

        Shipping

        Export documents ready; FOB, CIF or DDP.
