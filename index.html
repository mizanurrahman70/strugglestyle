How an order comes together

From your first brief to delivery at your port — nine clear steps, one team.

  1. We review your brief, tech pack and target price, then confirm costing and a realistic timeline.

  2. Patterns and tech packs are refined, and key styles can be previewed on 3D avatars.

  3. Fabrics, trims and accessories come from approved, traceable suppliers.

  4. Proto, fit and pre-production samples are made and approved together with you.

  5. Lines are planned and materials in-housed against a confirmed schedule.

  6. Optimised marker planning keeps fabric waste low before precise cutting.

  7. Garments are sewn on dedicated lines with inline quality checks.

  8. Washing, pressing, final AQL inspection and packing to your specification.

  9. Export documents are prepared and goods ship FOB, CIF or DDP.
